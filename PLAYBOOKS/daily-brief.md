# Playbook · Daily brief

Owner agent: `briefing`.

**Something comes in** — morning trigger (cloud routine, 7:00/7:30 AM IST depending on account framing).

**Brain picks the job** — Briefing agent aggregates.

**Reads what it knows** — today's + tomorrow's events on whichever account is actually connected (cloud routines can't drive local Chrome to switch it), unread/important Gmail from ~24h.

**Helpers do the work** — auto-accepts pending invites (flagging conflicts), applies triage labels, no sending/replying/deleting, posts one message: decisions needed → inbox summary → today's calendar.

**Safety checks** — if the connected account doesn't match the routine's framed account, says so at the top rather than reporting the wrong inbox as if it were right.

**Sign-off** — one consolidated message; P1s lead.
