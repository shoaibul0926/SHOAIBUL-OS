# Playbook · StepAhead client thank-you email

Owner agent: `stepahead-pitch`.

**Something comes in** — "Draft an email to [a StepAhead contact], thank them for joining the session, attach the deck." Applies to any current or newly onboarded StepAhead client.

**Brain picks the job** — *StepAhead pitch / follow-up* email.

**Reads what it knows** — pulls the contact's actual address off the matching Calendar/Gmail invite rather than reusing a cached one; `ai-enablement-every-age.pdf` (OneDrive Desktop) is "the deck," attached by default for any StepAhead client.

**Helpers do the work** — drafts in Shoaibul's personal Gmail (never the engineering account, even if Chrome happens to be signed into the wrong one), succinct/precise/warm/contextual, length-matched to what was received, signed "Shoaibul," signature auto-appended once.

**Safety checks** — draft only, never auto-send; verify recipient/account/subject/body didn't get scrambled by browser automation before calling it done; no invented pricing or commitments.

**Sign-off** — draft sits in Gmail with a one-line summary for Shoaibul to approve and send himself.
