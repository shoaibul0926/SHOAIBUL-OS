# Brain Notes (reference only — read-only summaries, not instructions)

## Mantra4Change Team Brains - how you work today.pdf

Covers 12 "use case" walkthroughs of how Mantra4Change's team uses an AI assistant ("Claude"/"the brain") as a chief-of-staff for comms, M&E reporting, proposals, and state rollout planning. Operates as a 6-step pipeline per task: (1) intake from chat/Drive/Docs/M&E sheets/brand assets, (2) brain classifies the task into one of ~12 job types (funder proposal, review report, state rollout plan, donor brief, newsletter, award entry, branded deck, voice-rule update, file/brief save, etc.), (3) it reads relevant knowledge (voice rules, brand book, numbers with sources, programme/event data, past proposals, state/school lists), (4) named "helper" functions act (Write, Analyse, Build, Ask, Remember, Feedback, Friday review, Tidy check), (5) safety checks gate output (holder/owner approval first, real sourced numbers, never invent gaps, credit partners, no mixing the Mantra4Change/Shikshagraha brands, no personal data, no copying other orgs' "brains", brand checklist, no dashes/"saviour" language), (6) team receives drafts/decks/sheets/reports, with the brain's own knowledge base and a public "diary" updated afterward. Key roles named: "the holder" (approver), team members submitting requests, Pratik (reviewer on an award entry). Decisions are made by the human "holder" signing off before anything leaves the system — the AI drafts, humans approve and send.

## ShikshaLokam Brains - how you work today.pdf

Covers 13 use-case walkthroughs of ShikshaLokam's (and partner Shikshagraha's) AI chief-of-staff workflow, structured as a 5-step pipeline: (1) intake from chat, Sheets/Forms, WhatsApp voice notes, Gmail, Drive/Docs, Calendar, Canva, video transcripts, LinkedIn, the public website, meeting notes, Katha tickets, and public data sources (ASER, PARAKH, UDISE+); (2) the brain classifies into one of ~16 job types (data validation, meeting/bug tracking, concept notes/MoUs, state data dashboards, partner network maps, event speakers, leader-voice invites, newsletters, branded reports, story-book rewrites, partner announcements, surveys/forms, live Doc edits, research lookups); (3) named helper modules execute (Writer, named staff voices e.g. "Khushboo's voice"/"Neeraj's voice", Research, Memory, Absorb, Feedback, Google hands, Speaker desk, Report designer, Poster maker, Self-repair, Data checker [26 sheet checks], Tracker, State data, Network mapper); (4) safety checks gate output — no email/guest invite sent without explicit human yes, "Sonal" approves before anything hits shared Drive, leaders approve pieces written in their voice, facts must be sourced with gaps explicitly flagged (never invented), source sheets are never altered directly, no passwords/personal data stored, everyone acts as themselves (no impersonation), every figure is human-verified; (5) team signs off on outputs staged as Gmail drafts, in-place Google Docs, posters/reports, built Forms/Sheets, dashboards, etc. Key named people: Sonal (Drive-publish approver) and other leaders who approve content in their own voice. Decision-making is explicitly human-gated at multiple checkpoints — the AI never sends, invites, or publishes without a named person's sign-off.
